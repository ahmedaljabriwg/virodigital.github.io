What it does

Everything to ship a landing page, in one plugin.

Each capability below is included in the free version. ViroBuilder uses your own Anthropic and Pexels API keys, stored only in your WordPress database.

✦

AI-designed pages

Describe your business in a sentence and ViroBuilder designs a complete page — layout, sections, and copy written for your offering. Different businesses get different layouts.

🖼

Real photos, auto-placed

Pexels integration finds and places relevant stock photography in the page. Click any image in the editor to swap it for another.

💬

Chat & click editor

Refine the page in a visual editor: click any element to change its text, image, or link, or describe a larger change in plain language.

🔍

SEO on every page

Generated pages include meta tags, Open Graph and Twitter cards, and JSON-LD structured data, plus semantic headings.

📱

Responsive output

Pages are built with responsive layouts across desktop, tablet, and phone breakpoints, which you can preview in the editor.

📥

Lead-capture forms

Forms on your generated pages save submissions to your dashboard, so enquiries are collected in one place.

The method

From a sentence to a page, in four steps.

Generation typically takes a couple of minutes — the AI is designing the page from scratch, not assembling a template.

1

Describe

Enter your business name, what you offer, and an optional industry, audience, location, and brand color.

2

Design

ViroBuilder calls the Anthropic API to write the copy and design the layout, then places real photos from Pexels.

3

Refine

Open the visual editor. Click to edit text, images, and links, or chat to make broader changes.

4

Publish

Publish to your site. Pages are saved as standard WordPress content — clean, portable HTML you own.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to know how to code? +
No. You describe your business in plain language and edit the result by clicking elements or chatting. The output is standard HTML if you ever want to work with it directly.
What do I need to run it? +
WordPress 6.2 or newer, and your own API keys: an Anthropic (Claude) key for generation and a Pexels key for photos. You enter them in the plugin's settings, and they are stored only in your own WordPress database.
How long does a page take to generate? +
Usually a couple of minutes. The AI designs the page from scratch — writing copy and building the layout — rather than loading a fixed template, so it takes a little longer than picking a theme.
What happens if I deactivate the plugin? +
Your pages remain — they are saved as standard WordPress content. You would lose the AI chat editor for further automated edits, but the published pages stay on your site.
Who makes ViroBuilder? +
ViroBuilder is built by Viro Digital LLC, a U.S.-based IT and WordPress development company in Connecticut, specializing in WordPress, accessibility, and secure hosting.
